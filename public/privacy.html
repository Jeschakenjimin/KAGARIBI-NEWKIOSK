Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

Welcome to the Kagaribi Japanese Grill digital ordering kiosk. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to being transparent about how we handle your information.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum amount of data required to fulfill your food order:

  • Order Details: The food and drink items you select.
  • Service Type: Whether you are "Dine-In" or "Take-Out."
  • Table Number: To ensure our staff delivers the food to the correct location.

2. Payment Security

We do not store or process credit card numbers or banking details on our servers. All digital payments are handled through secure, third-party mobile wallets (GCash or Maya).

3. Data Retention

Order data is stored temporarily to facilitate kitchen preparation and is automatically cleared after the business day ends. We do not sell or share your order history with outside marketing companies.

4. Your Rights

As a customer, you have the right to ask our staff about the data being collected at any time. Since we do not require accounts or logins, we do not store long-term personal profiles.
